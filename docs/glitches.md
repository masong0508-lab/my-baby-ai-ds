# Escalating corruption, bad endings and deliberate reset

**Tone:** intentional bargain-bin DS baby-game oddities, slapstick corruption, never jumpscare horror.

* Tier 0 (0–19 corruption): normal awkward baby.
* Tier 1 (20–39): brief visual distortion.
* Tier 2 (40–59): every third arcade directional prompt explicitly marked REVERSE.
* Tier 3 (60–79): reversed prompts, response window reduced from 120 to 75 VBlanks.
* Tier 4 (80–100): additional facial corruption and difficult but signposted arcade twists.

Four endings currently exist: Bottle Subscription Ended (hunger), Baby CEO Quit (mood), Mandatory Daycare Reboot (corruption + chaos), and Unpaid Intern Revolt (chaos). SELECT advances the day and records conditions. After 3 **different** endings or 5 total bad endings, hold L+R in nursery to enter the special ending, **Erase My Memories, Not Your SD Card**.

No ending or glitch wipes automatically. On the uncorrupted confirmation screen press A, then hold L+R and press X. B cancels at either step. Only `fat:/my_baby_ai.0` and `fat:/my_baby_ai.1` are deleted. The two checksummed alternating save slots are used to survive interrupted writes. When storage is unavailable the app can play without persistent saves.

**Safety:** No strobe flashing, hardware damage, storage-formatting, threats to unrelated files, or unannounced loss of progress. Reset remains an optional irreversible in-universe gag.

**Status:** working first gameplay prototype, *not* 16 bespoke minigames, a trained neural net, an emulator-verified production game, or hardware-benchmarked 60 FPS. All of those remain milestones.
